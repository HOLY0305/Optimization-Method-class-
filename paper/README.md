# Course paper and figure sources

[English](README.md) | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

The PDF is the complete single-column version of the course paper. Its six in-paper figures are supplied as editable SVG and 300 dpi PNG files under figures/svg/ and figures/png/.

| Paper figure | Asset name | Source figure | Notes |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | fig01_modeling_and_analysis | Figure A | Modeling, alternating Lloyd updates, and analysis overview |
| 2 | fig02_capacity_assignment | Figure B | Four-point capacity assignment; in-figure title removed |
| 3 | fig03_gaussian_perturbation | Figure C | Gaussian configuration and interface perturbation; in-figure title removed |
| 4 | fig04_empirical_hit_fraction | Figure E | Saved strict pre-terminal hit fractions and Wilson intervals; in-figure title removed |
| 5 | fig05_conditional_hit_iterations | Figure F | Conditional hit-iteration medians and bootstrap intervals; in-figure title removed |
| 6 | fig06_high_mode_trajectories | Figure G | Selected saved high-mode trajectories |

The PNG files are raster previews of the corresponding final figure artwork. The SVG files preserve editable vector elements. Figure D was not part of the paper and is not included.
